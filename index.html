About our Founder

Laura Shanley is a growth-oriented executive, educator, and board member with 20 years of experience driving strategic transformation across technology, retail, and education. Laura spent 12 years at Amazon and AWS, where she held P&L responsibility for multibillion-dollar business units, managed $100M+ budgets, and led global teams. She built and scaled the global marketing organization for Amazon's first ultrafast delivery business Prime Now, spearheaded marketing transformation across Amazon's omnichannel grocery business, and established enterprise-wide product lifecycle governance frameworks at AWS. She is an adjunct faculty member at NYU Stern, where she teaches Growth Strategy and AI, Building and Innovating in Omnichannel Marketing, and Professional Responsibility in Tech.

Today, as Founder of Court Street Advisors LLC, she provides strategic counsel to C-suite leaders on business growth and scaling mechanisms, go-to-market, and AI transformation. Laura brings three key elements to engagements: proven execution at the executive level, mastery of world-class operating mechanisms (PR/FAQ, working backwards process, product lifecycle frameworks), and cross-industry perspective from teaching at NYU Stern. She partners with executives to build sustainable growth engines and high-performing organizations.

Our Approach

How we work

Strategic Diagnosis and Framework Development

We diagnose complex challenges and design solutions grounded in proven frameworks. Every engagement begins with understanding your context, including competitive position, organizational capabilities, and growth ambitions. We bring perspective from Amazon/AWS operating mechanisms, cross-industry benchmarking, and hands-on operational experience to help our clients make better decisions at critical inflection points.

Operating Model Building

We design structures, processes, and mechanisms that create effective, agile organizations. Drawing from proven frameworks like PR/FAQ, working backwards methodology, and enterprise-wide governance systems, we help you establish operating models that eliminate silos, create clear ownership, and align cross-functional teams around strategic objectives.

Advise Through Execution

We help design the roadmap, and we stay through implementation. We run workshops, write playbooks and templates, and work with your tooling teams to build the process into existing systems. Our role is to help clients build organizational capacity and realize immediate returns.
