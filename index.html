About our Founder

Laura Shanley is a growth-oriented executive, educator, and board member with 20 years of experience driving strategic transformation across technology, retail, and education. Laura spent 12 years at Amazon and AWS, where she held P&L responsibility for multibillion-dollar business units, managed $100M+ budgets, and led global teams of 90+ professionals. She built and scaled the global marketing organization for Amazon's first ultrafast delivery business (Prime Now), spearheaded marketing transformation across Amazon's omnichannel grocery business, and established enterprise-wide product lifecycle governance frameworks at AWS. Today, as Founder of Court Street Advisors LLC, she provides strategic counsel to technology companies and nonprofits on digital transformation, go-to-market strategy, and business growth and scaling mechanisms. She partner with executives to build sustainable growth engines and high-performing organizations.
