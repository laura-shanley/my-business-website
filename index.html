About our Founder

Laura Shanley is a growth-oriented executive, educator, and board member with 20 years of experience driving strategic transformation across technology, retail, and education. Laura spent 12 years at Amazon and AWS, where she held P&L responsibility for multibillion-dollar business units, managed $100M+ budgets, and led global teams of 90+ professionals. She built and scaled the global marketing organization for Amazon's first ultrafast delivery business Prime Now, spearheaded marketing transformation across Amazon's omnichannel grocery business, and established enterprise-wide product lifecycle governance frameworks at AWS. She is an adjunct faculty member at NYU Stern, where she teaches in both the Marketing Department and Business and Society Program.

Today, as Founder of Court Street Advisors LLC, she provides strategic counsel to companies and nonprofit organizations on digital transformation, go-to-market strategy, and business growth and scaling mechanisms. Laura brings three key elements to engagements: proven execution at the executive level, mastery of world-class operating mechanisms (PR/FAQ, working backwards process, product lifecycle frameworks), and cross-industry perspective from advising technology companies and teaching at NYU Stern. She partner with executives to build sustainable growth engines and high-performing organizations.

Our Approach

How we work

Strategic Diagnosis and Framework Development

We partner with C-suite executives to diagnose complex challenges and design solutions grounded in proven industry frameworks. Every engagement begins with understanding your context, including competitive position, organizational capabilities, and growth ambitions. We bring perspective from Amazon/AWS operating mechanisms, cross-industry benchmarking, and hands-on executive experience to help our clients make better decisions at critical inflection points.

Organizational Design and Operating Models

We design structures, processes, and mechanisms that create effective, agile organizations. Drawing from proven frameworks like PR/FAQ, working backwards methodology, and enterprise-wide governance systems, we help you establish operating models that eliminate silos, create clear ownership, and align cross-functional teams around strategic objectives. We equip your leadership team with the frameworks needed to drive transformation.

Executive Advisory and Change Management

We provide strategic counsel informed by real-world experience. Our role is to help clients look around corners and identify risks, challenge assumptions, and provide competitive benchmarking that informs strategic direction. We design solutions and partner with clients to build mechanisms that drive continuous improvement in their organizations.
